# Fonts

All fonts in this folder are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org) and are self-hosted so the site does not depend
on Google Fonts at runtime. Each file is cut down (subset) to the characters
the site uses; the licence and copyright names are kept inside every file.

The faces were chosen by measuring the label's own posters (letter widths,
stems, counters) against the candidates on Fontsource.

| File | Family | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `league-gothic.woff2` | League Gothic, width axis (The League of Moveable Type) | The names on the series posters: artist names, mix numbers, dates, the names of the nights, lineups joined by dots |
| `tenor-sans.woff2` | Tenor Sans (Denis Masharov) | All reading text; the KIARASH poster lettering |
| `lalezar-fa.woff2` | Lalezar (Borna Izadpanah) | The posters' Persian: Persian dates (digits and month names) |

The name and the section words are not a font: they are painted with the
brush of the logo (see `assets/brush`).
