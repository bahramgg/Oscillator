# Fonts

All fonts in this folder are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org) and are self-hosted so the site does not depend
on Google Fonts at runtime. Each file is cut down (subset) to the characters
the site uses; the licence and copyright names are kept inside every file.

The faces were chosen by measuring the label's own posters (letter widths,
stems, counters) against the candidates on Fontsource.

| File | Family | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `six-caps.woff2` | Six Caps (Vernon Adams) | The compressed headline face of the 2026 event posters: section names, NO SIGNAL, the Output line, event and form titles, the menu |
| `league-gothic.woff2` | League Gothic, width axis (The League of Moveable Type) | The names on the series posters: artist names, mix rows, lineups joined by dots |
| `teko.woff2` | Teko, weight axis (Indian Type Foundry) | The boxed series numbers: every number on the site |
| `lalezar-fa.woff2` | Lalezar (Borna Izadpanah) | The posters' Persian: Persian dates (digits, month and weekday names) |
| `tenor-sans.woff2` | Tenor Sans (Denis Masharov) | All reading text and labels; the KIARASH poster lettering |
| `anton-ring.woff2` | Anton (Vernon Adams), the letters of OSCILLATOR/ | The hero's rotating ring, as in the "Oscillator Rises" reel |
| `orbitron-title.woff2` | Orbitron (Matt McInerney), the letters of WE ARE OSCILLATOR | The hero line under the ring, as in the reel |
